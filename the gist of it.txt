WorldWrapper:
- stores map data
- provides helper functions for raycasts and collision checks between entities and map tiles and areas
- provides a list of entity spawners
- updates entities that are active in it to ensure collision works properly. (generally, idle players will be in the Hub, meanwhile active players and monsters will be in the GameBoard)
- clientOnly:
	* renders shit
	* collision and raycasting are shared checks

MyRoom:
- reconciles players network-wise
- ticks WorldWrapper and FaunaWrapper
- network rewinding

FaunaWrapper:
- handles updating entities relative to themselves and to each other
- classifies entities (player, monster, particles, etc.)
- handles remote event from the players
- clientOnly:
	* renders shit
	* entities updates also happen on the client

Entity:
- a point in space with a rendering client-only tick and a behavioral shared tick
